# Hollywood Shoe — Delivery Outcomes

- [ ] **Premium Home experience:** Deliver a real working Hollywood Shoe Home page with an original, light-luxury campaign identity; use premium visuals, restrained cinematic motion, minimal text, a strong typography system, and a clean warm-white / ivory / cream / subtle-gray presentation that is neither a dark luxury theme, generic ecommerce template, gaming site, nor generic AI layout.
- [ ] **Navigation and future destinations:** Provide exactly HOME, MENS, WOMENS and KIDS in the primary navigation. HOME must correctly represent the current Home page. MENS, WOMENS and KIDS must have functional, clearly marked future-destination placeholders and must not pretend their shopping pages or shopping experience are finished. Do not add unrelated primary navigation items.
- [ ] **Campaign-first composition:** Build an immersive campaign sequence with a cinematic hero, multiple premium advertising/editorial campaign sections, a discovery-focused Trending Shoes experience, a full-width campaign, a working premium video moment with fallback, featured shoe visual storytelling, a final brand moment and a minimal footer. Keep content visual-led with sparse copy; do not build cart, checkout, payments, accounts, filters, complete product details, or full shopping flows.
- [ ] **Trending discovery interactions:** Provide image-led trending shoe cards or slides with generous whitespace, refined hover effects for desktop, touch interaction for mobile, keyboard-accessible controls, smooth transitions and minimal product context. Products must not be directly purchasable and must not show cart or purchase controls.
- [ ] **Responsive premium interaction:** Deliver intentional mobile, tablet, laptop, desktop and large-screen layouts with responsive navigation, touch spacing, image proportions, video behavior, no horizontal overflow, no overlap, no cropped critical content, no broken layouts and no unusable navigation. Include controlled fade/scale/reveal/parallax motion, smooth scrolling and reduced-motion support without excessive animation.
- [ ] **Media, quality and verification:** Use optimized/lazy media where appropriate; ensure images and video load correctly with fallbacks; ensure animations run smoothly; verify initial load, navigation, hero, video playback, campaign sections, trending interactions, scrolling, hover/touch interaction, desktop/tablet/mobile layouts, image loading, performance basics, route manifest and console/runtime errors. Fix observed defects before delivery.
